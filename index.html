What is Morona?

Morona is a financial intelligence platform that brings all your bank accounts, cards, payments, and transfers together in one smarter app. It does not replace your banks. It makes them work smarter together.

How does Morona make my money smarter?

Morona connects your bank accounts, understands your financial activity, and helps you make smarter decisions. From choosing the right account for payments to spotting spending patterns and planning ahead, Morona makes your money work smarter.

Is Morona a bank?

No. Morona is a financial intelligence platform that connects to your existing banks. Your money stays in your bank accounts, while Morona brings everything together in one intelligent app. Morona doesn't take deposits or lend money.

How is Morona Card different from a normal bank card?

A normal bank card uses one account. Morona Card connects multiple bank accounts, automatically chooses the best account for each payment, and can combine balances when one account isn't enough. One card, all your money.

What can I ask Morona Vision?

Almost anything about your money. Ask where your salary went, how much you can spend this month, whether you can afford a purchase, or how to reach your savings goals. Morona Vision understands your finances and gives you personalized answers in plain language. It never moves money without your approval, and it doesn't give investment advice or make credit decisions.

What does Morona Insights do?

Morona Insights brings your financial activity across all your banks into one clear picture. It tracks spending, income, bills, and subscriptions, spots patterns and changes, and helps you understand where your money goes.

What makes Morona Transfer different?

Morona Transfer lets you move money between your bank accounts, send money to others using their mobile numbers, and make international transfers — all from one app. No switching between banking apps.
